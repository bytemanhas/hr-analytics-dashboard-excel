# hr-analytics-dashboard-excel
Built an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard in Excel using Pivot Tables, VLOOKUP, KPI calculations, slicers, and dynamic visualizations. Analyzed workforce, attendance, recruitment, salary distribution, and attrition trends across 500 employees to support data-driven HR decision-making.


# HR Analytics Dashboard | Excel

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based business intelligence solution designed to provide actionable insights into workforce performance, recruitment effectiveness, attendance trends, compensation distribution, and employee attrition. The dashboard enables HR teams and business leaders to monitor key workforce metrics, identify patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project simulates a real-world HR environment with employee, attendance, recruitment, and attrition datasets integrated into a centralized reporting solution.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often struggle to consolidate workforce data from multiple sources into a single reporting system. Without a centralized dashboard, monitoring employee performance, hiring efficiency, attendance behavior, and attrition trends becomes time-consuming and inefficient.

This dashboard addresses these challenges by transforming raw HR data into meaningful KPIs and interactive visualizations that support strategic workforce planning.

---

## Objectives

* Monitor workforce composition and employee distribution.
* Track attendance and productivity metrics.
* Analyze recruitment performance and hiring success rates.
* Identify attrition patterns and employee turnover drivers.
* Support HR decision-making through interactive reporting.
